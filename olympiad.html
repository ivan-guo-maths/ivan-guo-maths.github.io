International Mathematical Olympiad

I served as a member of the Problem Selection Committee (PSC) and as a Coordinator for the International Mathematical Olympiads (IMOs) from 2023 to 2026. I was Chair of the PSC and Chief Coordinator for IMO 2025 in Australia.

The PSC selects, vets and refines problems from international submissions to produce the annual IMO Shortlist. Coordinators work with team leaders to assess contestants’ solutions and agree on scores, ensuring consistent and fair marking.

I was the Deputy Leader of the Australian IMO team from 2009 to 2013 and served as Team Leader in 2011.

As a student, I represented Australia at IMO 2003 (Japan) and 2004 (Greece), achieving a Bronze Medal in 2003 and a Gold Medal in 2004.

Australian Olympiad mathematics

I have been a member of the Australian Mathematical Olympiad Committee (AMOC) since 2008.

Since 2020, I have been Chair of the AMOC Senior Problems Committee (SPC), having been a member of the SPC since 2007. The committee sets the Australian Mathematical Olympiad (AMO) Summer, Winter and Final rounds, and prepares problem proposals for the IMO, European Girls’ Mathematical Olympiad (EGMO) and Asian Pacific Mathematics Olympiad (APMO).

For many years I have taught at Olympiad camps, including the AMOC Schools of Excellence and IMO Selection Schools, and mentored students on the Australian IMO Team.

I received the Australian Maths Trust’s BH Neumann Award in 2018 for contributions to mathematics enrichment.
